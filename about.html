OUR STORY

One truck.
A lasting commitment.

Founded in Dubai, we began as a family-run business with one truck and a clear goal: make deliveries safer, more dependable, and easier for our customers.

Today, Raja & Tahir Transport serves businesses moving foodstuff, steel and accessories, tires, construction materials, and portable cabins. Our route experience across the UAE and Oman helps us plan each journey with care.

Behind every load is a team focused on your cargo, your schedule, and the details that make a delivery work.
